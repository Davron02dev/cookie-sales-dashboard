# Cookie Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing cookie sales performance — customer orders, cookie types, and profitability.

## Overview

This dashboard explores sales data across customers, order volume, and cookie product types, surfacing key metrics like total profit, profit margin, and order distribution by weekday to help understand what drives sales performance.

## Features

- **Customer analysis** — cookies sold and number of orders broken down by customer
- **Profitability view** — total profit and profit margin % by cookie type
- **Order patterns** — distribution of orders by weekday
- **Product performance** — treemap of total profit contribution by cookie type
- **Interactive filtering** — year slicer for time-based analysis
- **KPI cards** — total orders, distinct customers, total profit, cookies sold, and profit margin at a glance

## Tools & Techniques

- Microsoft Power BI
- Data modeling (Customers, Orders, Cookie Types tables)
- DAX measures (profit, profit margin %, distinct counts)
- Visuals: donut chart, column chart, bar chart, area chart, treemap, pie chart, KPI cards, slicer
